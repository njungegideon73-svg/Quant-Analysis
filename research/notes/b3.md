## Concept definitions (merged)

### Swing high / swing low
- Says: never defines a fractal; calls them "the highs and lows" of the move, then uses "valid"/"validated" ones. Key rule: a low is only a (higher) low that counts after the market has pushed to a new high ("if we don't create a new higher high, then the higher low is not going to be valid" - ygleB1CLhUE [10:07]). In a downtrend the high that created the break of the last low is the structural high.
- Proposed: pivot with lookback N (e.g. N=3/5 bars each side) on HIGH/LOW; "structural" swing = the extreme that *produced* a BOS (the last opposite swing before the impulse), not every pivot. Parameter: N, TF. Keep two sets: all pivots (internal) and BOS-anchored pivots (structural).
- Ambiguity: which of many pivots count is chart-by-chart. He "averages out" price action and skips pivots that don't fit. Not rule-defined.

### Market structure / trend state
- Says: HH+HL up, LL+LH down, else consolidation; trend flips only when the last valid HL (uptrend) is broken by close -> lower low. [ygleB1CLhUE 04:03, 29:18-30:18]
- Proposed state machine: state UP with `last_HL` = lowest low between the swing low preceding the latest HH and that HH; UP->DOWN when a bar CLOSES below `last_HL` (body-close, param `close_beyond=True`); symmetrically DOWN->UP above `last_LH`. "Consolidation" = after a wick-only break of both sides or while price is inside [last valid low, last valid high] without a closing break (state NEUTRAL; no trades).
- Ambiguity: his own chart shows "in real time" a corrective move that later became a lower low; the labels (impulse/correction/consolidation) were drawn with hindsight and he admits it (ygleB1CLhUE [18:10]). Whether the break needs "large body" or any close is unspecified.

### BOS / "CHoCH"
- Says: BOS = every time a prior high/low is broken *by closure*; he labels both continuation and reversal breaks BOS and calls CHoCH "nonsense terminology" (ygleB1CLhUE [24:14]). Wick-only = sweep/consolidation. Candle closure is "a large candle body ... close through" (ygleB1CLhUE [39:23]).
- Proposed: BOS_up when close[i] > last_structural_high (price level of that swing high); optional `min_body_ratio` (body/range >= 0.5) and/or `min_body_atr`. Tag as continuation if prior state UP, else reversal. A wick break with close back inside = SWEEP event, not BOS.
- Ambiguity: "large" body undefined; one close or two? In _gvbOxMgt0Y [22:22] "as long as the candle body closes... doesn't matter if there is a wick", i.e. any close suffices. In Ep.9 he treats "break and close" and "engulfing" as the shift.

### Internal vs external (swing) structure; structure layers
- Says: internal = everything inside the range of the latest impulse; breaks there aren't trend changes (ygleB1CLhUE [33:20]). Separately "swing structure" = HTF directional bias (e.g. 4H); "substructure" = trend on a lower TF inside the HTF correction (30m inside 4H; 15m inside 4H; hourly swing / 15-5m sub). Internal highs/lows are also liquidity (DwEl2fjqGNw [09:11], UGivqBXO28I [15:14]).
- Proposed: compute structure on two TFs (HTF swing, LTF sub). HTF bias from HTF state machine. LTF "substructure" signal = LTF BOS against HTF bias while inside HTF correction; allowed profit zone = [LTF entry, last HTF opposing swing extreme (previous leg's extreme / nearest HTF demand or supply)], never past the HTF last LH/HL.
- Ambiguity: which TF pair (ratios used: 4H/30m = 8x, 4H/15m = 16x, 1H/15-5m, 15m/5m ~3x). "Internal" is also used loosely for LTF structure. He says "counter trend doesn't exist" (LSs9femocqc [03:02]) while elsewhere calling it counter-trend.

### Impulse vs correction
- Says: impulse = leg that breaks a prior swing (creates HH/LL); correction = pullback that stays inside the impulse's high-low. If the next high is equal, not new, the push + pullback + push is one impulse (DwEl2fjqGNw [09:11]).
- Proposed: leg from swing pivot to next opposite pivot; impulse if its extreme exceeds prior same-side swing (closing or wick? he uses closing for BOS but impulse by price extreme), else correction. 
- Ambiguity: leg boundaries drawn by eye.

### Supply & demand zone / order block
- Says: zone = "the last candle before the impulse" (or small consolidation) that broke structure; for demand: last bearish/sideways candle before the push up (ygleB1CLhUE [46:29]; DwEl2fjqGNw; FUC0iWyCI08 "candle before the large up move"). Must contain *imbalance* (unfilled gap after it); once a wick taps the zone it is "retested"/used up. Demand only in uptrends, supply only in downtrends. Zone stop = beyond far side (below low of demand/above high of supply). Zone is invalidated ("failed") if a candle CLOSES beyond it (UGivqBXO28I [08:06]). Prefer the zone whose impulse broke the most prior lows/highs ("more significant").
- Proposed: for a bullish BOS leg, zone = last down-close candle (or last N<=3 small-body candles, `consol_max_bars`) before the first candle of the leg; zone high/low = candle high/low (body vs wick option; he uses wicks for stop: "above the zone and the wick"). Valid if FVG exists between zone candle and a later candle (see below) and no later bar has traded into zone (`fresh=True`, wick-touch = used) and no close beyond. Rank by number of prior swings broken by its impulse.
- Ambiguity: wick vs body for zone bounds; consolidation (multi-candle) vs single candle; "last candle" colour; whether the imbalance must remain unfilled to *trade*, or just at creation; "significance" is eyeballed. In Ep.7 "demand/internal low" are interchangeable POIs - unspecified priority. Wick-tap invalidates in UGivqBXO28I but entries are literally taps; contradiction resolved only by "first tap".

### Imbalance / FVG
- Says: imbalance = "open candle range": gap between wicks, i.e. a region not traded by neighbouring candles; filled when a wick trades into it (UGivqBXO28I [09:07]; ygleB1CLhUE [47:29]).
- Proposed: 3-candle FVG: bullish if low[i+1] > high[i-1]; gap = [high[i-1], low[i+1]]; filled when later low <= gap upper bound (partial vs full via `fill_threshold`). Zone has imbalance if a FVG begins at/adjacent to the zone candle in the impulse direction.
- Ambiguity: "touch" vs "fill 50%/fully" never specified; he says a wick into the zone "retests" the zone, i.e. mixes zone and gap.

### Efficient vs inefficient range
- Says: efficient = every demand/supply zone and every gap inside the leg has been retested/filled, so no POI left; price entering an efficient range is unlikely to reverse and likely to continue the prior direction (UGivqBXO28I [08:06]-[11:07]). "Range is efficient, at bare minimum we get to the high" (DwEl2fjqGNw [21:22]).
- Proposed: for leg A->B, `unfilled_fvg_count(leg)==0` and no unmitigated zones => efficient; use as continuation filter / target-extender. Parameter: lookback = last impulse leg.
- Ambiguity: used both as "price will drift through" and as "no reversal fuel"; conflates two claims. No test.

### Liquidity: EQH / EQL, relative equal highs
- Says: 2+ highs (or lows) at about the same price; exact or "relative" equal. 4 perfect + 3 relative on AUDUSD (jJKYeglSjHw [09:03]). Orders sit above (stops, breakout buy stops, retest limit buys). Price is "magnet"; "never sell below equal highs, never buy above equal lows" (-UIrmQ3y0nI [19:09]; UGivqBXO28I [11:11]). 
- Proposed: cluster of >=2 pivots (N-bar) with |h_i - h_j| <= tol where tol = k*ATR (k about 0.1-0.25) or X pips, within max_age bars. Sweep: bar high > cluster max by >= epsilon and close back <= cluster (wick sweep). Filters: block shorts if unswept EQH within d ATR above entry (and symmetric).
- Ambiguity: tolerance, number of touches, min spacing, lookback - all eyeballed. Also contradicts himself: "never sell below EQH" vs trade sold from supply below equal highs (3rd trade, UGivqBXO28I, a 6R short from supply with no EQH above - fine) but in -UIrmQ3y0nI the post-sweep supply "worked" only after the EQH sweep, with an early near supply he calls "bad". Also uses EQL as target for shorts (sell into EQL), consistent but means rule is directional.

### Liquidity sweep vs break
- Says: sweep = price pokes beyond swing high/low, no body close beyond, sharp reversal; break = body close beyond (ygleB1CLhUE [39:23]; _gvbOxMgt0Y [23:25]). After a sweep: do not enter immediately; wait for LTF structure shift (FUC0iWyCI08 [11:07]).
- Proposed: SWEEP at bar i: high[i] > L and close[i] <= L (single-bar) or close back inside within `m` bars (default 1-3). BREAK: close[i] > L. Post-sweep filter: BOS on LTF within `k` bars.
- Ambiguity: price can close beyond *then* reverse ("trendline: market closed above with strength" yet it was a trap - qErVtn3NBbQ [13:10]) - the same candle pattern is called break or trap depending on later bars. So sweep/break distinction is only knowable ex-post for those cases.

### Inducement
- Never uses the term. Closest: internal highs/lows inside an impulse as "fuel"/sweep targets before the move (DwEl2fjqGNw [09:11]-[11:13]; UGivqBXO28I [15:14]) - treat as internal liquidity sweep before POI.

### Trendline liquidity
- Says: trendline is not a trend indicator; it's an area where stops/breakout orders pile. Break/close beyond it often reverses; real trend defined by last structural LH/HL. Use: (a) sell supply (or buy demand) in the pocket between trendline and "true structure point"; (b) target for extension.
- Proposed: trendline = line through >=2 (3 preferred) swing pivots; "trendline break" event = close beyond line; take only if structure state unchanged. Must be implemented as pivot-regression with fixed rules; liquidity zone = band within `m` ATR of the line.
- Ambiguity: trendline drawing is discretionary (which pivots, wick/body, slope); he says tool is "arbitrary" yet uses it to extend targets.

### Premium / discount

- Proposed: if desired, equilibrium = 0.5 of last impulse leg (parameter), longs only below, shorts above. Not his rule.

### Candle momentum / multi-candle momentum
- Says: body size relative to neighbours (explicitly "subjective dependent on other candles"); wicks = rejections; engulfing of many candles = strong; multi-candle phases: strong run, indecision, reversal (Xhhx6havGac, _gvbOxMgt0Y).
- Proposed: body_ratio = |C-O|/(H-L); upper_wick_ratio, lower_wick_ratio; high momentum: body_ratio >= 0.7 and body >= m*ATR; indecision: both wick ratios >= 0.3 and body_ratio <= 0.3; momentum reversal: dominant wick >= 2x body at a POI. Phase momentum: rolling mean of signed body_ratio over n bars; "fast down, slow up" = ratio of bars (or time) taken for down leg vs retrace leg (e.g. retrace bars >= 2x impulse bars while retracing <100%).
- Ambiguity: no thresholds; "who is in control" is read holistically.

- Ambiguity: which TF's close (LTF for confirmation, signal TF for zone failure).

## Complete trade setups / entry models

**A. Structure + supply/demand continuation (ygleB1CLhUE, mFmEkEEKJQE, BNVGhjxP4JY)**
- Bias: HTF (any TF; weekly->daily->4H in his flow) trend by HH/HL or LL/LH with close-based BOS. Only demand in uptrend / supply in downtrend.
- POI: last candle before the BOS impulse, with unfilled imbalance; choose the *extreme/last-imbalance* zone ("lowest possible"/highest one).
- Trigger: price returns to zone. Entry: limit at zone (mFmEkEEKJQE "sell limit at the extreme"; ygleB1CLhUE "buy orders here") - no confirmation, or optional confirmation (B).
- Stop: beyond zone or beyond swing high/low (often "above the high that created the zone"). Target: previous swing low/high; extend optional. RR is structural, not fixed (examples: -1R, +3.94R, +5.42R; 5% and 10% "refined"). Win rate shown 2/3 on 3 trades.
- Pairs/TFs: NZDJPY 15m, EURCHF/AUDJPY (BOS drawing), XAUUSD 4H/30m, EURGBP 1h/1m/daily. Sessions not specified.

**B. "Standard confirmation" entry (ygleB1CLhUE, Xhhx6havGac, FUC0iWyCI08, DwEl2fjqGNw)**
- Bias/POI as in A (HTF zone). When price reaches zone, drop 1-2 TFs and require LTF shift: for a sell, HH+HL then a candle CLOSE below the last HL (LL), then entry on the LH (the supply/pin bar/doji created at top) - sell on the retest of the LTF supply, stop above the LTF swing high (or HTF structure, safer), target previous low.
- ygleB1CLhUE example: 10m idea, 5m confirmation, "5% vs 10%+ refined". DwEl2fjqGNw: 4H context -> 1H confirmation (close above LH after sweep of internal low) -> buy demand that made the shift; stop under the sweep low; target supply, 4.5R estimated / 4.8R realised; min 3R rule.
- Management: partials/BE/trail mentioned, not specified. Fixed RR? No: structural, with >=3R filter.

**C. Liquidity-sweep 3-step system (FUC0iWyCI08, -UIrmQ3y0nI)**
1. Identify EQL/EQH or range (swing) high/low liquidity; wait for sweep.
2. LTF structure shift in new direction (e.g. close above the last LH after EQL sweep; or engulfing "reversal of the pull up" for the short).
3. Place limit at fresh LTF demand/supply created by the shift (refine on 10m/15m if invisible); stop under the full sweep low / above zone; target next liquidity (swing high, EQH, or opposite zone).
- Claimed: RRs ~3-5.5R on 7 charts, trades in a range chain (buy then sell). Win rate claimed "5 of 10 is fine", RR structural (target nearest liquidity). Sessions: not specified (one 5m/10m range day; Asia range mention in zb_kiI-VVwU).
- Variation (-UIrmQ3y0nI): in a downtrend, skip the closest supply sitting under EQH; wait for EQH sweep then sell the supply at premium after the sweep ("sell supply after liquidity's been taken").

**D. Trendline-liquidity trap (qErVtn3NBbQ, jJKYeglSjHw)**
- Bias: structure intact (last LH holds). Identify descending trendline with >=2 taps. The trap area is between trendline and true structure point (last LH).
- POI: supply (last candle before impulse; imbalance) inside the pocket. Entry: sell limit in zone, stop above the true structure point, target the prior low. Same mirrored for buys.
- Variant: buy demand toward trendline after an internal bullish BOS (3R, 3.5%) - trendline as magnet target.
- Claimed outcome: shown 1-2 examples; extension to 3.4R.

**E. Substructure (sell-to-buy) trade (ygleB1CLhUE, LSs9femocqc, BNVGhjxP4JY)**
- HTF swing bias (4H up); HTF buy zone (demand with imbalance) is the final destination. While price is above it, wait for an LTF (30m/15m) BOS down (candle close under LTF HL) -> take LTF short from fresh LTF supply with imbalance (sell limit or on close under the low), stop over LTF high, target = HTF demand (previous leg's extreme / last imbalance), never beyond the HTF swing extreme/last LH. At HTF demand: take profits / BE and then buy with the HTF trend with the stop below the low and target HTF highs.
- Fixed RR? No. Win rate claimed implicitly 100% on 2 examples (survivorship).
