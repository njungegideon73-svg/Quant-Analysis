### Swing high / low
- Said: highs/lows of price used to read trend; "significant" low = the low that caused the BOS (the pivot at the start of the move). Quote: "the final lower high in the trend meaning essentially the final point of which the market was trending down" [02:00 4Ymh5Rvm0C8].
- Objective: fractal pivot with lookback N (suggest N=2-5 left/right bars), high[i] > max(high[i-N..i-1]) and > max(high[i+1..i+N]). "Significant" = pivot that is the extreme of its leg (zig-zag with min move of k*ATR, e.g. k=1.5-3).
- Ambiguity: no N, no minimum size; "significant swing", "notable high" are eyeballed. Uses wicks and bodies interchangeably.

### Imbalance / FVG / inefficiency

## Complete trade setups / entry models

### 1) Confirmation entry (standard confirmation), reversal and continuation [4Ymh5Rvm0C8, Z4Y301e_udQ, 9nRFcetXqkA]
- Bias: HTF context (reversal: HTF zone + key level, continuation: HTF trend and pullback). Wait for a LTF downtrend (LL/LH) then BOS up by close above last LH.
- POI: demand = last candle before the impulse that caused the BOS (with wick). Needs imbalance into it (untapped).
- Entry: buy limit at/just above zone (+0.2 pip), placed as soon as BOS prints. Short mirrored.
- Stop: under swing low of the confirmation (alternatively under zone). Target: previous swing high / next opposite supply. Fixed vs structural: structural, but he screens for >=3R.
- TFs: daily->4H, 1H->30m, 5m; pairs: NZDUSD, NZDCAD, EURCHF, others. Claims "highest probability", no numbers.

### 2) Continuation trade, two entry types [RvO3iQjXui0]
- Bias: structure trend; require recent BOS; imbalance (open price range) between the high and the zone must be FILLED before entry.
- POI: supply/demand zone, last candle before impulse. Entry A: limit at zone. Entry B: wait for LTF fractal (HH,HL,HH -> LL -> first LH) then limit at zone.
- Stop: above previous LH (A) or just above zone/LTF high (B). Target: previous swing low / unfilled demand; typically 3R+, shown 8.53R and 3.37R (A) vs 9.31R and 5.38R (B).
- Mgmt: no BE; avoid stacking positions. Frequency: A catches ~all, B ~half, B higher win rate (asserted).
- Pairs: AUDCAD 1H.

### 3) Imbalance-fill entry [D6svZ1whG7U, 4J-kl3c4KlM]
- Bias: HTF structure. Identify extreme unmitigated zone; wait for price to fill all imbalance and tap the zone ("no more imbalance"), then sell/buy at zone. No trading until imbalance fully closed.
- Stop beyond zone (aggressive: near-term imbalance; conservative: beyond swing). Target: next open zone/imbalance in direction, or earlier-left imbalance (supply) as counter-trend target.
- Example 5.6R; UJ buy 3.75% vs 1.2% market buy; EURUSD 3%.

### 4) Efficient range break-through [YYW3yPfyHpg]
- Bias: bullish/bearish trend with a chain of retested (efficient) zones plus one last imbalanced zone.
- Trigger: price takes out the last imbalanced zone's low (the failed buy) -> go short through the efficient range (sell supply on pullback/LTF entry) -> TP range low (range liquidity). Then look for a buy flip at range low. The first buy at the last demand acts as setup whose failure triggers the second trade.
- Stops: above the break. RR structural. Win-rate claims: none; "not always reversal".

### 5) Liquidity sweep + BOS "fakeout" [MObFIEX6hXs]
- Bias: HTF (4H) resistance/support with many touches (5 touches), expecting sweep.
- Trigger: sweep above, LTF (1H) sweeps high, breaks + closes below previous low; sell supply (last candle before impulse including wick) with stop above the high; TP = next demand (1.9115 AUDCAD? shown level only). Conservative: wait until 4H closes under range low, sell pullback to HTF supply.
- RR: aggressive 2x profit of conservative, which is "more likely to win"; no numbers.

### 6) Zone-selection filters + inducement avoidance [9nRFcetXqkA, fwISomH5qNY]
- Pick the extreme unmitigated zone with limit; take confirmation entries at inner zones; require zone on HTF; prefer zone that broke structure with strong impulse.
- Example EURCHF 5m: skip two inner demand zones (no confirmation, imbalance beneath), buy extreme demand after BOS + push into efficient range, target next supply; ~3 hour trade.

### 7) Supply/demand fail flip [psBNHz8oTIQ]
- Setup: planned buy at fresh demand; price closes through it. Flip: sell pullback into nearest higher supply, TP next demand (or swing low). Early version: sell after 3rd tap into the demand; or buy at supply after repeated taps and strong close at top.
- Stop: above the supply zone; ~4% in one example, no hit-rate given.
