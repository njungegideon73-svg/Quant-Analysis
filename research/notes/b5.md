**5. Candlestick reading (Candle Theory)** (990oOC-bHxA)

- "Multi-candle momentum" = speed: up move done in 15 hours vs 4 days to retrace; 3 days of selling erased in 2x30m candles. Faster side is stronger.
- Indecision candle before a strength run = demand/supply zone; control-shift candle at a POI is the higher-probability buy.
- Confirmation by closes above a notable structure point. Worked example is a long (buy from control-shift/indecision zone after a "weak" 9-hour pullback).

## Concept definitions

### Swing high / swing low
- Says: structure is "waves", not every candle; pick "clear viable" highs/lows. "Viewing the structure as waves... zoom out" (jWKT-P9pPdc, 08:05).
- Objective: pivot high = bar whose high is the max over N bars left and right (N parameter; 2-5 for substructure, 5-10 for swing). Alternatively zigzag with minimum retrace of k*ATR(14) or a threshold of % of the previous leg. Parameters: N, k, timeframe.
- Ambiguity: no N or size rule is ever given. "Clear" is eyeball. Different count per video (he marks highs/lows differently when "inducement" or "liquidity range" matters, 4jc5myA2lgw 04:04 "kind of meaningless to me because it's a liquidity range").

### Market structure (trend state) / BOS / CHoCH-equivalent
- Says: uptrend = HH+HL, downtrend = LL+LH, else consolidation. Break = body close beyond the last structural level; "validate a higher low" before reversal (jWKT 13:08). He uses "break of structure" for both continuation and reversal; also "market structure shift", "standard confirmation".
- Objective: from confirmed pivots, state = UP if last two pivot highs and lows ascending, DOWN if descending, else RANGE. BOS (continuation) = bar close beyond the last same-direction swing extreme. Reversal/shift = bar close beyond the most recent counter-trend swing (last LH in a downtrend); valid only after subsequent HL (confirmation variant: require an HL pivot, which costs N bars of delay). Parameter: close vs wick (he says close), min body close distance (none given, 0).
- Ambiguity: does the break need the candle to be "strong" ("not impulsive or strong enough", ewZVjxk0SlY 24:00)? Not defined. "Wick = rejection" but he treats wick-only breaks as "invalidating" levels elsewhere (ewZ 24:00 "we did start to see willingness to go higher"). Which pivot counts as the structural level is picked per case.

### Internal vs external (swing vs substructure), structure layers
- Says: swing structure = HTF trend; substructure = smaller trends inside corrections; substructure trades should not target beyond the swing structure's relevant extreme high/low (jWKT 15:08-17:09, "only look to target below this High").
- Objective: compute pivots on two timeframes or two N values (N_big, N_small). Counter-swing trade allowed only if entry-LTF structure state is opposite to HTF state; target cap = last HTF swing extreme (the LH). Fractal "any timeframe" claims (vCh4so18bDg 10:11).
- Ambiguity: which timeframes pair is up to him (4H/30m, 4H/1H, 30m/15m). The cap rule is stated once and elsewhere he takes full targets.

### Supply & demand zone / order block
- Says: zone = "last candle before the impulse" (not last opposite candle); impulse = move creating an imbalance; extend zone to cover a following wick if that wick is a pre-impulse low (vCh4so18bDg 31:26-34:28). In other videos: "indecision candle (equal wicks, small body) before strength candles" (990oOC 14:11; _dcYd 04:04); sometimes "consolidation range" with multiple candles (vCh4so18bDg 14:11, ewZ 26:16 "broad range as a point of supply").
- Objective: for each bar i, find i = last bar before the first impulse bar j where impulse = body >= m*ATR(14) (suggest m=1.5) OR creates a 3-candle FVG (high[j-1] < low[j+1] for bullish). Zone = [low_i, high_i] (wick-inclusive), optionally extended to the low of bar i+1 if bar i+1 wick breaks below i and still holds. Must be same direction as the trend filter. Zone is "fresh/imbalanced" until price trades back into it; first tap only.
- Ambiguity: three different definitions across videos (single candle, consolidation box, wick-extended). "Impulse" has no size threshold. When the very first candle of the move is itself impulsive he steps back, but "step back how far" is not defined. Reports that students called him inconsistent (34:00).

### Imbalance / FVG, efficient vs inefficient range
- Says: imbalance = open price range left by an impulse (gap between wicks) not yet retested (vCh4so18bDg 31:26; BPJec 11:04). Zone with imbalance = "imbalanced zone" = a magnet; "any small amount of imbalance" counts (4jc5 04:04). Efficient = every zone in a phase has been retested; inefficient = untested zone left (ewZ 17:09-18:10). The "extreme" zone is the last imbalanced zone before an efficient range.
- Objective: FVG = candle 1 high < candle 3 low (bull); track filled = later bar's low <= FVG bottom (full) or any touch (partial; choose). Zone-level imbalance = zone not revisited since formation (any wick touch of the zone range). Efficient range = no unmitigated zones between two swing points. "Extreme zone" = the unmitigated zone furthest from current price within the current swing leg (leg = last confirmed swing low to swing high).
- Ambiguity: "retested" means touch vs penetrate vs close inside is never stated. Gap/imbalance size minimum not given. Whether a half-filled imbalance still counts is unspecified.

### Liquidity: EQH/EQL, sweep vs break, inducement, trendline liquidity
- Says: EQH/EQL = repeated tests of same level; stop-loss and breakout stops pooled beyond; "do not buy above equal lows, do not sell below equal highs" (KnYg 08:04); price is drawn to them (magnet) then reverses or continues; "relative equal highs" (ewZ 05:02); "inducement" mentioned by name only (ewZ 24:15, "we're going to get into soon"); trendline liquidity is NOT covered in this batch (trendlines are dismissed as a tool in jWKT 04:01-07:04).
- Objective: EQH = two or more swing highs within tolerance t (e.g. 0.1*ATR or X pips) of each other; pool level = max of cluster. Sweep = bar trades beyond pool level but CLOSES back inside (wick beyond, close not beyond); break = body close beyond. Test at the bar close of the chosen timeframe (he insists on waiting for candle close: D7Hu 16:13-19:15). Rule: block longs when an unswept EQL lies within d below entry (d undefined; use zone-bottom distance); after sweep + body-close break of a prior swing high, permit entry.
- Ambiguity: no tolerance for "equal". "Sweep vs break" is called by context (fuel, imbalance, demand beyond) so same price action is labelled reversal or sweep depending on what he sees. He explicitly says a close beyond doesn't settle it in an expanding range ("you can't really trust every break here", D7Hu 13:11). Inducement never defined.

### Premium / discount
- Says: fib with 0/50/100 on the current leg; buy only in bottom 50%, sell only in top 50%; "never buy above the 50% mark and never sell below" (cd_nbyc 25:15). Zone straddling 50% = "right structural shifts" needed. Pick the most extreme zone for direct limits.
- Objective: leg = last confirmed swing low to swing high (reverse for shorts). EQ = (high+low)/2. Zone eligible for longs if zone high <= EQ (strict) or zone midpoint <= EQ (loose). Parameter: leg-definition (pivot N), strict vs mid.
- Ambiguity: which leg (nested legs give different 50%s), whether zone high or midpoint must be below 50%, and in ewZ 24:15-28:17 he SELLS in a buyer-dominated trend from a premium zone against HTF structure, which contradicts "only trade with trend".

### Candle momentum / multi-candle momentum / candle closures
- Says: strength = large body, small wick in direction; flat tops/bottoms strongest; control shift = single large wick, body colour irrelevant; indecision = both wicks, small body; "multi-candle momentum" = how fast a move covers distance compared with the pullback (15 hours up vs 4 days down; 3 days erased in an hour). Closure rule: structure breaks need a body close, wicks are rejection (jWKT 20:13; KnYg 10:06; _dcYd 16:10).
- Objective: body_ratio = |C-O|/(H-L); strength if body_ratio >= 0.7 (param) and directional wick <= 10% of range; size >= 1.2*ATR. Pin/control shift: one wick >= 2/3 of range (param) or >= 2x body. Doji: both wicks >= 25% each and body_ratio <= 0.3. Momentum: speed = ATR-normalised distance / bars; compare impulse-leg speed vs retrace speed ratio >= r (param, he gives none).
- Ambiguity: no numeric thresholds anywhere; body-size relevance is "relative to recent candles". "Candle momentum" is qualitative. Contradiction: wicks "don't matter" for breaks but are central for control-shift candles.

### Accumulation / distribution / Wyckoff
- Not used by name. Closest: "consolidation... buyers accumulating" (990oOC 21:15), "expansion range" with alternating HH/LL, and the "Asia range" as a liquidity range. No rules.

### Fakeouts
- Same as sweep (see above). "Failed shift" / "supply fail" / "demand fail" are his terms. Supply fail = price breaks above the supply zone high. Objective: close or wick (he accepts wick: ewZ 14:08 "even if just with a wick") above zone high -> zone invalid, flips bias. Ambiguity: wick vs close inconsistency between supply-fail (wick OK) and structure break (close needed).

### Fibonacci
- Only the 50% line (premium/discount). No OTE / 61.8 / 78.6 retracement levels or extensions in this batch.

### Session timing
- Says: UK-time windows Asia 01:00-07:00, London 08:00-12:00, NY 13:00-17:00; London push ~07:00-08:00, NY ~12:00-14:00; trades should be flat by ~17:00 UK; "80 to 85% of days" lose direction after 17:00 (xIbYGU318j0 19:12).
- Objective: Asia range = high/low of 01:00-07:00 Europe/London bars. Sweep = London-session bar takes high or low and closes back inside range. Direction filter: after first-sweep side, trade toward the opposite extreme after LTF structure shift. Time exit at 17:00 UK. Parameters: window times, TZ handling (UK local time, DST-aware), instruments (majors only).
- Ambiguity: no stats, no sweep-then-reverse frequency, "very often enough for it to actually matter"; fixed windows rather than true session opens; excludes news days by eye ("nothing's going to avoid data").

### Timeframe alignment, top-down narrative
- Weekly -> daily -> 4H gives bias/POIs, 4H/1H/30/15m and lower (even 30s/1m) for entries. Bias is a conditional tree ("if this high breaks, buy; if this low breaks, sell") not a prediction. Objective: HTF state from pivots; LTF entries only when LTF structure agrees (standard confirmation). Ambiguity: which "weekly range" is picked is by eye; the tree mostly has two branches so every outcome is "predicted" after the fact.

### Stop placement
- Beyond the zone (beyond extreme of the candle +/- covered wick), or under swing low (supply: over zone high or swing high). "More conservative" variant: under the full previous low (KnYg 12:08). Never a fixed pip or ATR value; no buffer stated. Objective: stop = zone extreme +/- buffer b (b=0..0.2*ATR param). Ambiguity: zone vs swing stop chosen case by case.

### Targets

"Look to the left" rule. Objective: target = nearest unmitigated opposing zone edge or swing extreme in the trade direction. RR then falls out (2R-9R shown). ## Complete trade setups / entry models

**A. Trend + extreme supply/demand limit (core model)** (vCh4so18bDg, defSZ, ewZ, KnYg, BPJec)
- HTF bias: HH/HL (buy) or LL/LH (sell) via swing structure on the 4H/daily; HTF used only to filter side.
- POI: last candle before the impulse of an imbalanced (unmitigated) zone, in discount (buy) or premium (sell), "extreme" one furthest from price in the current leg, not above an unswept EQL (buy).
- Entry: limit at the extreme zone (aggressive) or, for middle zones, wait for LTF "standard confirmation" (LTF LL/LH -> body-close above last LH, then limit at the new LTF demand with imbalance).
- Stop: beyond zone (or beyond swing low).
- Target: nearest unmitigated opposite imbalanced zone / swing; scale out at each. Fixed RR: none; structural.
- TF: bias 4H/1H, entry 15m-30m, sometimes 30s/1m zones. Pairs: GBPUSD, EURUSD, USDJPY, gold in examples.

**B. Narrative "if this then that" (weekly -> 4H -> 1H)** (defSZ)
- Weekly range from last leg; daily/4H POIs and liquidity; bias set on weekend. Trigger is a break of a stated swing level; entry on resulting supply/demand; stop above/below zone/swing; target liquidity (equal lows) or opposing zone. 1% risk. 
- It is a framework rather than a specific rule: both directions are always prepped, trigger levels chosen visually.

**C. Liquidity-sweep reversal (EQL/EQH)** (KnYg, BPJec, D7Hu)
- Side: against a market that has just swept EQL/EQH (Asia range also counts). Trigger: sweep of the level, then body close beyond the last swing (BOS). Entry: limit at the demand/supply created by the BOS impulse. Stop: beyond zone or swing. Target: opposing supply or equal highs (4%; 8.9% aggressive).

**D. Sweep vs shift decision** (D7Hu)
- Not a trade by itself. Before taking a BOS/CHoCH entry, check the fuel: if the break runs into untested demand + EQL + imbalance, expect a sweep and fade (buy the sweep low) instead. Otherwise the break is real.

**E. Supply/Demand fail flip** (ewZ)
- If a zone is broken by price through its far edge (even wick), flip bias and treat that as a new leg; buy the next "extreme" demand, stop under the low, target the last imbalanced supply; or exit at the first imbalanced supply to avoid reversals.

**F. Premium/discount filter** (cd_nbyc) - applied to A: ignore any zone on the wrong side of the leg's 50%.

**G. Session sweep model ("Asia sweep, London push, NY reversal")** (xIbYGU318j0, 4jc5)
- Mark Asia 01:00-07:00 UK high/low. Wait for London to sweep one side, then LTF structure break the other way (4jc5 EURUSD: sweep above, LTF HH/HL then BOS down), sell from an imbalanced 30s/1m supply, target the Asia low. Exit before 17:00 UK. Time windows only suggested; he describes it as optional. RR 3.4R on one trade.

**H. Monday gap fill (rebalancing) and sentiment continuation** (_dcYd)
- Gap fill: gap = Friday close vs Sunday/Monday open. Trigger: price pushes back above the structural high (sweep) then BODY close below the structural low in the gap-fill direction. Entry: limit at supply (extreme supply gets a direct limit; middle supply requires repeating confirmation). Stop: over swing high. Target: gap-origin price (Friday close). 3.85%.
- Sentiment: after the gap fills, trade the original gap direction. Wait for body close beyond the previous structure, limit at supply/demand, stop beyond swing, target the extreme low/high. 6.4%. Explicitly "not tested" for the blind-limit version.

**I. Candle story entry** (990oOC)
- Buy trade at a demand zone formed by an indecision/control-shift candle, after a fast impulse and a slow pullback (multi-candle momentum), after control-shift wicks, then strength closes above a notable structure point. Discretionary - the video itself says "we would want to see some more strength candles" without defining it.

## Quant red flags
1. **Hindsight-only labelling**: "extreme zone", "efficient range", "current leg", "most recent leg of price action" all require knowing the leg's end. Swing points need N future bars; any code must delay confirmation by N bars or the backtest will leak.
2. **Zone definition drifts**: three S/D definitions (single last candle, indecision-candle, consolidation box) and wick-extension, with no impulse magnitude. Same chart yields multiple answers.
3. **Context overrides rules**: sweep vs break is decided by "fuel" (liquidity, imbalance, untested demand) judged by eye; in the same video the same break is bullish or a trap. Not codable without inventing thresholds.
4. **Counter-trend contradictions**: "never trade against structure" yet he sells within an HTF uptrend from premium (ewZ 24:15-28:17) and buys substructure inside a swing downtrend (jWKT). Rule says one thing, examples do another.
5. **Survivorship / selection**: every example is a winner with clean retest (2-9R), all hand-picked; one missed trade is shown as hypothetical 9R. "Two wins, one miss, no losses" week; "I avoided a lot of trades" means unobserved filtering. Any quoted % results (3.85%, 4.14% on $200k simulated, 6.41%) are single trades; one simulated account. Student payout claims and pitch material appear in several videos.
6. **Unreferenced stats**: gap fill "majority of the time", "80 to 85% of days", Asia sweep frequency; none are measured, and he says the gap-limit edge is untested and the Asia sweep is "not a holy grail".
7. **Stop/target are hindsight-friendly**: stops at "the low" and targets at "the next imbalanced zone / swing" are chosen after the move is visible; RR (3.4R-9R) is a by-product of target choice, not a fixed rule.
8. **Execution realism**: entries on 30-second and 1-minute zones, limit orders at exact zone edges (fills on touch, no slippage/spread), partial exits "to lock in" decided live. Backtest needs spread and fill rules; touching a limit is not equal to being filled with price continuing.
9. **Wick vs close inconsistency**: breaks need body closes, but a supply "fail" counts on a wick, and sweeps are by definition wick-only. A coder must choose per concept.
10. **Time filters tuned by eye**: UK-time windows ignoring DST for US/Asia sessions; skipping news days by "feel".
