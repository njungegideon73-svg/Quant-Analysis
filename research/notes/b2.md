## Concept definitions (merged)

### Swing high / low, structure (HH/HL/LH/LL)
- Says: trend = HH+HL or LL+LH; "we are looking at the real highs and lows" [0kl4, 05:07]; view structure as waves so "you don't get caught up in every tiny move" [14:12]. Highs/lows must be "validated" by a BOS.
- Proposed: fractal pivot with lookback N bars each side (N=2..5 per TF) with a minimum-leg filter (e.g. swing leg >= k*ATR(14), k~1-2) to enforce the "waves, not tiny moves" idea. Pivot confirmed N bars late.
- Ambiguity: no N, no size filter. "Real", "significant", "internal" highs are eyeballed. He calls a low "not entirely valid higher low" in structure terms yet trades it [3jtK, 44:25]. Blunt: swing selection is discretionary.

### Break of structure (BOS) / CHoCH
- Says: BOS is when the previous high/low breaks and gives way to new price action; the same word used for continuation and for the first break against trend (reversal). "Change of character... for me that's kind of pointless terminology" [0kl4, 05:07]. Needs "solid closures"/candle body close, not wicks [hkyG, 09:04; 0kl4, 10:10].
- Proposed: bull BOS when close[i] > last confirmed swing high (parameter: close vs wick; he prefers close; optionally require body-close beyond by >= x*ATR). Reversal BOS = first such break against the prior trend state (state machine: bearish while sequence LH/LL holds).
- Ambiguity: which swing counts ("significant high" [hkyG, 08:04]); whether one close suffices ("a few solid closures" [0kl4, 14:12]); closes vs "momentum candles closing above" (IHH_ not specific). In 3jtK he says some traders would call a shift a BOS and sell, but context should override - i.e., BOS is not self-sufficient.

### Substructure vs swing structure / structure layers / internal vs external
- Says: swing structure = impulses on the higher TF; substructure = corrective moves in the opposite direction, which are themselves trends on the LTF [8QZ1, 01:02]. "Internal" low is a swing inside a leg [3jtK, 54:32]. Trailing stops must use entry-TF structure [0kl4, 11:10]. Max two TFs down for refinement [m-G1, 15:15].
- Proposed: run the same pivot algorithm at 2-3 TFs (trade TF, TF x4-6, TF/4). "Substructure" = the structure state of the trade TF while the higher-TF state is the opposite; trade only when trade-TF state == signal direction (and optionally HTF state).
- Ambiguity: he alternates between "trade only with the trade-TF trend" and "never fight HTF (mistake #2)" and "daily zones only for daily sells" [8QZ1, 04:03]. No rule for which wins when they conflict.

### Supply & demand zone / "order block"

"Zone that broke structure" is preferred [hkyG, 11:06].

Untouched (unmitigated) until first retest; invalid once a candle closes through the far edge.
- Ambiguity: "last candle" vs "sideways cluster" ("area of consolidation" [m-G1, 00:00]) are used interchangeably; "impulse" has no size threshold; how many candles to include; whether to use wick or body for zone edges; stop "under demand" vs "under swing low" is chosen case by case [IHH_, 15:07 vs 22:12]. ### Zone retest strength / fails

### Imbalance / inefficiency (his version of FVG) / efficient vs inefficient range

## Complete trade setups / entry models

**A. Reversal model (IHH_eKHt9uo; hkyG relates)**
- Bias: prior trend ends; first structure break against trend (LH/LL sequence broken by a close above last LH -> bullish BOS).
- POI: the last (nearest-to-price) *imbalanced* demand within the new leg: imbalance above it must have been filled first; zone drawn as last candle before the impulse. Prefers context: reversal arrives at an HTF demand [IHH_, 12:05-13:06].
- Trigger: none beyond the structure break (limit order resting). Entry: buy limit at demand (top of zone or tap).
- Stop: below swing low (the reversal low) or below demand when efficient range below.
- Target: prior swing high / imbalanced opposing supply zones (up to 5 stages); RR structural. Claims: 4.2R on 5M (London, 2h20), 11% on 6H EURUSD over a month.
- TFs: 6H, 5M shown; "works on all". Pairs: EURUSD plus unspecified 5M pair.

**B. Fakeout / liquidity sweep reversal (hkyG6bxI93U)**
- Bias/location: price sweeps a swing low (or high) on the hourly with a wick, no close beyond, or a fast reversal closing back above prior highs.
- Confirm: structure break on the TF with *body closes* beyond a significant swing high; LTF/HTF rejection candle supports it. "No trade until BOS".
- Entry: buy limit at demand that broke internal structure (from LTF refinement), or at 71-78% fib of the confirming leg.
- Stop: below the swept low. Target: liquidity / opposing supply zone. Claims: 4.5% in a couple of hours, other trade took days. 
- Breakout counterpart: close beyond range/high, pullback to S/D zone in trend, target next high.

**C. Supply/demand retest with zone refinement (m-G1ag77aVc)**
- Bias: BOS from LL/LH into HH/HL on 4H (NZDJPY example).
- POI: HTF (4H) demand -> refine to 1H (max 30M) zone inside it; if several, limit at "extreme" (lowest) zone; upper zones only after LTF "standard confirmation" (LTF new high after tap).
- Entry: buy limit at refined zone top/extreme. Stop under the low of leg (HTF zone low). Target recent swing high.
- RR: HTF 3.08 -> 1H 5.58 -> 30M ~6 ("sniper"); hypothetical 1.9 -> 6.25R diagram; 5M claimed 25R but "often missed".

**D. S&D failure / flip (ldNlF-FZm7E)**
- Context: trend; demand tapped but price closes through zone (or forecast: second tap deeper than first reaction, no new high, body-close in zone).
- Entry: sell at the supply created by the failure (last candle before the breakdown impulse), or sell the tap ahead of the break; stop above zone; target next unfilled demand (after efficient range / imbalance).
- Variant: supply fails (price pushes through) -> buy the small demand on 15M toward next supply (~2.5R, refined 3.5%); or buy through equal highs forecasting the break.
- Fixed RR? No. Structural.

**E. Pullback entry with trend (Cnvk, k9li, nwWn)**
- Structure trend + HTF context; wait for a pullback into S&D zone ("don't chase"). No details beyond generic.

**F. Momentum cancel / filter (IwdC3M4NJtA)**
- Pending order at supply; cancel if a candle closes with huge bullish body engulfing many bars into the zone (momentum against); then look to buy the opposite demand. No numerical rule.

**G. Narrative scenario trade (3jtKtxBFJbQ)**
- Bias via weekly->daily structure/rejection; 4H: supply + unfilled imbalance + internal high liquidity; Scenario 1: price taps supply and shifts structure down on LTF (closes beneath low) -> sell, stop above high, target low/demand. Scenario 2: price breaks the high -> look for buy confirmation toward next supply. Entry: confirmation (market after structure shift) or limit at supply.
- Also two demand buys at equal-low liquidity in an uptrend (USDJPY) after sweep into demand.
- Not backtestable as described ("narrative").

**Management** (0kl4): BE after first BOS, structural trail after each BOS; aggressive candle trail near the target; avoid weekend gaps and news.

## Quant red flags

1. **Zone selection is hindsight-prone.** "Last candle before the impulse" is only known once the impulse has happened. Many candidate zones exist; he pre-selects the one that is later tapped ("the zone that broke internal structure" in hkyG; "extreme zone" in m-G1). Backtest must define zones strictly from bars up to time t.
2. **Swing points and "validated" structure need future bars.** Pivot confirmation lag (N bars). His trail rule ("low validated when high is broken") is OK in lag form, but chart examples mark highs/lows with full knowledge.
3. **"Impulse", "significant", "strong", "weak", "big wick", "choppy", "linear"** have no thresholds. Momentum video is entirely visual; each comparison uses completed legs.
4. **Refinement increases RR by moving the entry deeper; fill probability not accounted.** Refining from 4H to 30M zone yields 3R -> 6R on the same stop, but the stop-placement is unchanged and he admits "market isn't always going to reach those areas" [m-G1, 14:13]. Backtest must model non-fills and "tap and run" (extreme-zone limit stopped if price goes through). Zone-edge entry and stop gets in at best tick: spread/slippage matters at small stops.
5. **Survivorship in examples.** All shown trades work; the failed ones appear only as the counterexample. Stats like "4.2R", "11%", "4.5%" are single hand-picked trades; "$14,681 balance" is a simulation, with a weekend gap that happened to help/hurt.
6. **Optimized-by-eye targets.** Targets are chosen "wherever you want", sometimes the structural extreme; implicit lookahead of what price reached in the chart.
7. **Narrative layer (3jtK) is not codeable.** "Who is in control" mixes weekly rejection, momentum, and structure with discretionary override of BOS ("selling there makes no sense"). Alternate-scenario planning means every outcome is a trade - no falsifiable edge.
